What’s new in v3

Built to make coastal ops simpler

Live Vessel Tracking

Simulated AIS positions that move every 2 seconds, with statuses that shift between active and overdue.

Buoy Telemetry

Offshore buoys reporting battery, wave height and temperature — online/offline states shown on the map.

Working Alert Feed

Overdue, equipment and distress alerts with severity filters. Dismissing actually removes them — and logs it.

Weather Forecast

Hourly wind, wave and temperature outlook with a 7-day summary — simulated from local station data.

Advisories & Reports

Small craft and operational advisories, with catch-report entries, right inside the dashboard.

Activity Log

A time-stamped audit trail of every action — logins, vessel registrations and alert dismissals.
