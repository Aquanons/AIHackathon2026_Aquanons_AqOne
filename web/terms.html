1. Purpose

AqOne is an experimental maritime intelligence console developed for the Provincial Government of Aklan. It is a prototype that runs entirely in the browser on simulated sample data. Nothing in it should be used for real-time safety-of-life-at-sea decisions.

2. Accounts & roles

Accounts, passwords and role assignments are stored locally in your browser. Do not rely on this system to protect sensitive data. Three roles are available: Administrator, Operator and Viewer. Your role determines what actions are shown in the console.

3. Sample data disclaimer

All vessel positions, buoy telemetry, alerts, forecasts and reports shown are simulated or manually entered for demonstration. They do not represent real vessels or real government operations.

4. Acceptable use

Use this system only for training, demos and prototyping. Do not use it to impersonate officials, to claim official status, or to make operational decisions that could affect public safety.

5. Privacy

Because everything is stored locally, no data is transmitted to a server by this application. Clearing your browser data will erase accounts, reports and activity history.

6. No warranty

This software is provided "as is" without warranty of any kind. The developers accept no liability for decisions or outcomes resulting from its use.
