Regulator dashboard · AqOne v1.0 Aklan, Philippines

Welcome to the AqOne shared regulator dashboard. This platform supports the Bureau of Fisheries and Aquatic Resources (BFAR), Local Government Units (LGUs), and the Municipal Disaster Risk Reduction and Management Office (MDRRMO) of Aklan in their respective regulatory, planning, and emergency-response roles. By accessing and using this dashboard, you agree to the following terms.

Our commitment to decision-support AqOne is a decision-support tool only. All catch-decline flags, hotspot predictions, and hazard advisories are generated to inform human review. No automated restriction or enforcement action is taken by the system. BFAR and LGU retain sole authority over any fishery management measures.
1. User roles & responsibilities
BFAR / LGU — Access catch-monitoring tools, zone-flag review, and historical data. Responsible for evidence-based regulation and coordination with fishing communities.
MDRRMO — Access distress alerts, hazard advisories, buoy-triggered warnings, and vessel overdue check-in. Responsible for emergency response and rescue coordination.
Shared tools — Both roles see hazard banners, buoy zone advisories, and weather warnings. Role-specific panels are clearly labeled.
Account security — Each user must protect their credentials. All activities are logged; do not share your account.
2. Data stewardship & privacy
  • First-party catch data — Fishermen voluntarily submit geotagged catch logs. All data is anonymized before storage and used solely for hotspot prediction, trend detection, and regulatory reference.
  • Public & open data — We integrate NASA ERDDAP/PO.DAAC, PAGASA, OpenWeatherMap, NAMRIA, and BFAR municipal baselines under their respective open terms. No resale or redistribution.
  • Buoy & sensor data — Tilt, motion, and GPS from anchored buoys are used for hazard detection and mesh routing. Data is retained for safety analysis and is not shared with third parties.
  • Government oversight — BFAR and LGU may access aggregated, de-identified data for fishery management planning. MDRRMO may access real-time alert data for emergency response.
3. Acceptable use & compliance
  • Lawful purpose — Dashboard use must comply with all applicable Philippine laws, including the Fisheries Code (RA 10654) and local ordinances.
  • No misuse of alerts — Hazard warnings and distress signals are for official response. Do not trigger false alerts or misuse the SOS layer.
  • Decision authority — AqOne provides evidence, not enforcement. All regulatory actions (e.g., fishing cooldowns) must be formally declared by BFAR/LGU.
  • Timely review — Flagged zones and overdue vessels should be reviewed promptly. Delayed response may affect community safety.
4. Disclaimers & limitations
  • Connectivity-dependent — Offline mesh and LoRa improve coverage, but gaps exist. Do not rely solely on AqOne for critical safety decisions.
  • Predictive accuracy — Hotspot predictions are probabilistic; actual fishing conditions may vary. Always use local knowledge and weather advisories.
  • Hardware & maintenance — Buoy units are field-deployed; sensor drift or battery failure may occur. Regular inspection is recommended.
  • No liability — AqOne and its developers are not liable for decisions made by regulators or responders based on dashboard data. All actions remain the responsibility of the user agency.
5. Updates & support
  • Terms may be revised — We will notify users of material changes via dashboard banner or email. Continued use constitutes acceptance.
  • Support & feedback — For technical issues, data concerns, or policy questions, contact the AqOne support team or your BFAR/LGU focal person.
